# About and contact

The About route uses the owner-confirmed identity summary, stated technical interests, verified employment and education records. Education grades remain private. The philosophy and professional-direction drafts in the intake are not included in the profile data or page until the owner supplies approved wording.

The Contact route uses the confirmed email and verified GitHub/LinkedIn links. Email is the page's primary typographic element: an oversized, selectable `mailto:` link sits beside the write/copy actions. Copy feedback is announced through the existing status region. No contact form is used.

Verified profiles render as two ruled rows with handle-level URLs (for example `github.com/KnighhtRider`), not icon cards. The owner-provided resume is `public/resume/vivek_Thakur_Resume.pdf`, served at `/resume/vivek_Thakur_Resume.pdf`; the page offers an open-in-new-tab action and a direct PDF download when the file exists.
